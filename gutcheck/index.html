The Gut Check

Tipping shouldn't start with a percentage. The Gut Check asks a few plain questions about how the meal went and suggests a tip in dollars, so what you leave matches the experience you had.

How it works

  1. Enter the bill. Start with the total on the check.
  2. Say who's at the table. Add couples and singles in your party.
  3. Answer a few questions. Tell it how the food, service, drinks, and pace felt. Skip anything you don't want to answer.
  4. See your tip. Get a suggestion in dollars. Change the percentage yourself whenever you like.
  5. Split the check. For two or more people, see an even split, with each couple paying two shares.

Private by design

Every calculation happens on your phone. There's no account to create, and your bill and answers aren't sent anywhere or saved. Read the privacy policy for details.
