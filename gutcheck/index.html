Why it's different

A tip that reflects the meal you had

Most tip calculators hand you 15, 18, or 20 percent and leave the judgment to you. The Gut Check asks how the meal went and does the math from your answers.

  • Tip by feel, not formula

    Your answers nudge the tip up or down, and you get a dollar amount instead of a percentage to work out.

  • See what went into it

    The result lists every answer behind the number. If it feels off, slide it to whatever you want.

  • Splits that make sense

    Each couple pays two shares and each single pays one. Shares round up to the dollar, and the app shows how much that adds to the tip.

  • Private by design

    Everything runs on your phone. No account, no saved history, and nothing you enter leaves your device. Read the privacy policy.
