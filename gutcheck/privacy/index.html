Privacy Policy for The Gut Check

Effective October 2, 2026

The Gut Check is a tip calculator. This policy describes the Android and iOS app and this policy webpage.

Information used by the app

The bill amounts, party details, and answers you enter are used to calculate a suggested tip and, where available, a bill split. Those calculations happen on your device. The Gut Check does not send these entries to us, Google, advertisers, or anyone else. It does not save them on a server or keep a meal history. Your entries are cleared when you start over or close the app.

Advertising

The current app does not display ads or include the Google AdMob SDK. Its blank ad space is only a placeholder and does not collect advertising data.

If we add AdMob in a future version, Google and its advertising partners may collect the device's advertising ID when available and permitted, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, diagnostic information, and interactions with the app and ads, such as ad views and taps. They may use and share this information to serve and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. The bill amounts and answers you enter will not be sent to AdMob. See Google's Privacy Policy for more about Google's data practices. We will update the app store privacy disclosures when ads are introduced.

Advertising choices for European users

There is no advertising consent prompt in the current app because it does not request ads. Before we enable AdMob, users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland will see a consent prompt where they can accept, decline, or manage the use of their data for advertising. A Privacy choices option in the app will let them reopen the prompt later to change or withdraw that choice. Declining personalized advertising may still allow limited or non-personalized ads.

Purchases

The current app does not offer in-app purchases. If an Android version offers a purchase such as Remove Ads, checkout will be handled by Google Play. We will not receive your card number or other payment method details. The app may receive the product purchased, purchase status, and a purchase or order identifier needed to unlock or restore it. Google Play handles payment information under Google's Privacy Policy. We will update this policy if the purchase implementation uses or retains other information.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not receive or share the bill amount or answers you enter, and we do not keep a copy. The app has no account or stored meal history to delete. You can clear your current entries by starting over or closing the app. If advertising or purchases are added, the related data practices will be reviewed before release.

This webpage

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub may process information such as your IP address when you visit the page, including for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. This page does not use its own analytics or cookies.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. The latest version will remain available at this address.
