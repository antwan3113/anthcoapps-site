Privacy Policy for The Gut Check

Effective October 2, 2026

The Gut Check is a tip calculator. This policy describes the Android and iOS app and this policy webpage.

Information used by the app

The app uses the bill amount and the answers you enter to calculate a suggested tip. The calculation happens on your device. The app does not ask you to create an account, save your entries, or send them to us or a third party. Your entries are cleared when you start over or close the app.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not display ads and does not include advertising or analytics services. A blank space reserved for a possible future ad is not an ad and does not collect data. We will update this policy and the app store privacy disclosures before adding services that collect data.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not receive or share the bill amount or answers you enter, and we do not keep a copy. The app has no account or stored meal history to delete. You can clear your current entries by starting over or closing the app.

This webpage

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub may process information such as your IP address when you visit the page, including for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. This page does not use its own analytics or cookies.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. The latest version will remain available at this address.
